About Jhaz 1 Way Taxi

"Your Ride, Your Way." Reliable, transparent, and affordable one-way outstation and airport taxi services.

Tirunelveli's Trusted One-Way Outstation Network

Headquartered in Tirunelveli (Nellai), Tamil Nadu, Jhaz 1 Way Taxi was founded to eliminate unnecessary return-fare charges and unpredictable surges. We connect travelers directly with polite, experienced chauffeurs across South India.

Whether commuting from Tirunelveli to Madurai, Chennai, Coimbatore, Bangalore, Courtallam, or regional airports, our fleet of sanitized Sedan (Dzire), SUV (Ertiga), and luxury Innova Crysta cabs ensures safe, on-time journeys with transparent per-KM billing.
